Frequently Asked Questions

Which printer is best for students?

Inkjet printers are affordable and suitable for assignments, notes, and occasional color printing.

Which printer is best for offices?

Laser printers offer faster printing speeds and lower cost per page for high-volume printing.

Can I print from my phone?

Yes. Most modern Wi-Fi printers support Android, iPhone, AirPrint, and Google Cloud Printing alternatives.
